# Tolon 0.1.0-beta.1

The first beta of Tolon. Tolon watches your calendars and takes over the whole screen just before a meeting starts, so you join on time with one key.

This first beta is for macOS testers. The Windows and Linux installers are included, but no person has tested them on a clean machine yet.

## What is in this beta

- **Full-screen reminders.** Two minutes before a meeting, Tolon covers every screen, above full-screen apps too. Enter joins, Esc snoozes, D dismisses, Space marks you as already there.
- **One key to join.** Tolon finds the link for Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Webex and Jitsi, and falls back to any https link in the location. After you join, the call app gets the keyboard.
- **Your calendars, read only.** Subscribe to any iCal address: Google's secret address, a published Outlook.com or Yahoo calendar, or any `.ics` link. [How to get the link](https://github.com/danielpsf/tolon/blob/v0.1.0-beta.1/docs/help/ical-links.md).
- **Recurring events done right.** Repeat rules, exceptions, moved occurrences and time zones, including the night the clocks change.
- **Quiet when you want it.** Pause for an hour, set quiet hours, and ignore all-day events, events you declined, or titles with words like "Focus" or "Lunch".
- **A tray icon that knows what is next.** The next meeting and the rest of your day, one click away. Peek, the winner of the logo poll, is the icon.
- **Make it yours.** Light and dark, eight reminder colours, the optional Peek style, sounds, and 12 languages.
- **Private by design.** No account, no telemetry. Tolon only contacts your calendar links and GitHub, to check for updates. Every update is checked against the project's signing key.

## Known limits

- Tolon is not signed by Apple or by a Windows certificate authority yet, so macOS and Windows ask you to confirm it once. The steps are below.
- Google Calendar sign-in comes in beta 2. Until then, use Google's secret iCal address.
- Windows ships as the `-setup.exe` installer only; the `.msi` comes with 0.1.0.

Something wrong or confusing? [Open an issue](https://github.com/danielpsf/tolon/issues/new).
