## What the log changed

The owner's log (`~/Library/Application Support/dev.tolon.desktop/logs`, 2026-10-31 to 2026-11-06) settles two open questions from the review:

- At the 2026-11-02 13:59 alarm (12:59 UTC) two windows were raised, one per display. So F1 is not a missing window or a failed raise.
- Every alarm logs `could not leave the Regular activation policy error=setActivationPolicy failed with OS error 0x00000000`. Since L-068, Tolon becomes a Regular app while settings is shown and should return to Accessory before a reminder raises. That return fails every time, and a Regular app's window never enters another app's full-screen Space (L-042). This is the first suspect for F1, and it also explains why the earlier manual sign-offs passed: they ran before settings had ever been opened in that session.
- No `could not raise` or `could not place` line exists in the seven days kept. The 2026-10-29 log is gone (7-day rotation).
